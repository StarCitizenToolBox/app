### StarCitizenToolBox is open source software and does not collect any of your private information.
### 星际公民盒子 是开源软件，且不收集您的任何隐私信息。

#### Anonymous usage statistics

StarCitizenToolBox sends anonymous usage statistics (for example: a feature was used, and whether it succeeded, failed or was cancelled, together with a coarse error category and the app version). These events are tied only to a random install ID that is generated locally on first launch. No account information, IP-derived data, hardware identifiers or other personal data are collected. The statistics are used to measure feature success rates and retention. Per-install records are deleted after at most 1 year.

#### 匿名使用统计

星际公民盒子 会发送匿名使用统计（例如：某项功能被使用，以及其成功、失败或取消的结果，附带粗略的错误类别与软件版本号）。这些事件仅与首次启动时在本地随机生成的安装 ID 关联，不收集任何账号信息、基于 IP 的数据、硬件标识或其他个人信息。统计数据仅用于衡量功能成功率与用户留存。按安装 ID 记录的数据最长保留 1 年后删除。
